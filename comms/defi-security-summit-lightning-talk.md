## Outline (5 minutes, 5 beats)

**1. The paradox (~45s)**
- We spend so much on security, and yet $3.4B was stolen in 2025. 2026 isn't shaping up much better.
- *(Supporting: the Bybit hack alone was $1.5B, about 44% of 2025's total. North Korea took $2.02B, up 51% year over year. More money in, same losses.)*

**2. Why? We secure projects, not the ecosystem (~55s)**
- The network effects of security are incredible, but we forfeit them to the tragedy of the commons.
- *(Apartment analogy in one line: everyone puts cameras in their own apartment, nobody pays for the hallways. Duplicated audits, private monitoring, siloed OpSec. Wiring the whole building costs barely more than one apartment.)*

**3. Ostrom won a Nobel Prize solving this (~50s)**
- Elinor Ostrom showed communities can govern a shared commons themselves, without privatizing everything and without a boss dictating from the top.
- TheDAO Security Fund will take those lessons and add competition. Ostrom's coordination plus market competition for who secures the ecosystem best.

**4. TheDAO Security Fund is the coordinating institution (~60s)**
- QF round first, competitive RFPs now. This is already running, not a theory.
- *(Supporting: the proof is in 2025's data, where shared proactive monitoring and rapid response actually cut DeFi protocol losses even as TVL grew. Competitive RFPs now fund shared defense: formal verification of shared compilers and contracts, bytecode scanners on new deployments, shared OpSec.)*

**5. Call to action (~30s)**
- What is your project doing to secure itself? What if you could spend less by having others kick in too, and we scale it to the whole ecosystem instead of just your project?
- Submit an RFP. Or even better, fund one you would benefit from TODAY.
