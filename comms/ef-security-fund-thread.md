**1/**
Ethereum is growing up, and its security has to grow up with it.

EF Labs advances the protocol. EF Institutional brings serious capital on-chain. TheDAO Security Fund has its own focused job: make Ethereum safer than the banks. 🧵👇

**2/**
That goal sounds big, so let us show you how we think it actually happens. Start with a picture.

Imagine Ethereum as one big apartment building. Every project has its own apartment, and every team works hard to secure their own four walls.

**3/**
Now say one project, a busy DeFi team, decides they want security cameras. Smart move.

So they wire up cameras inside their apartment and outside their front door. Their hallway just got safer. The rest of the building is still dark.

**4/**
Here is the thing about cameras. Wiring the whole building costs barely more than wiring one apartment. The system is the hard part, and it is the same either way.

So that team is paying almost full price to protect one small slice of the building.

**5/**
This is where TheDAO Security Fund comes in. We go to that team and say: instead of building a camera system for your apartment, let's build one for the whole building.

We get other projects to chip in, so you pay less than you would have alone.

**6/**
Everyone comes out ahead:

• The team that wanted cameras spends less
• Every other apartment is covered too
• The whole building is safer, not just one hallway

Less money in. More security out. For everyone in the building.

**7/**
This is just how security economics work. Security has huge economies of scale and strong network effects.

The cost of protecting one project and the cost of protecting the whole ecosystem are much closer than they look.

**8/**
Right now Ethereum overspends on security, because every project fights alone in its own silo. Dozens of teams each paying near full price for cameras that only watch their own door.

Pool that same spend into shared infrastructure and it buys far more safety.

**9/**
Picture formal verification that covers every contract, scanners watching new bytecode the moment it deploys, shared OPSEC and monitoring for the whole ecosystem. Built once, protecting all of us.

That is a level of safety no single team can buy on its own.

**10/**
So here is our pitch. You are already spending on security. Spend it together.

Bring your security ideas to TheDAO Security Fund and we will help you scale them to all of Ethereum, for a fraction of what it costs to go it alone.

**11/**
This is how Ethereum becomes safer than any bank. A whole ecosystem watching each other's backs, funded together and secured together.

Come build it with us. 👇
