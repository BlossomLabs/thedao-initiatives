**Effective September 6, 2026.**

**1.3 The Site.** TheDAO publishes Initiatives and receives donations through the website at fund.thedao.fund (the "Site").

**1.4 No tax-exempt status.** TheDAO is a foreign nonprofit entity organized in the Republic of the Marshall Islands. **1.5 Contact.** Contact details are in section 14.

## 2. Acceptance of These Donation Terms

**In short: donating means you accept these Donation Terms, whether you use the Site or send assets directly.**

**2.1 Donations through the Site.** By donating through the Site, you agree to these Donation Terms.

**2.2 Direct transfers.** These Donation Terms also apply to any transfer of digital assets you send directly to an Initiative's multisignature wallet address, whether or not you use the Site interface to do it.

**2.3 If you do not agree.** If you do not agree to these Donation Terms, do not donate.

**2.4 Capacity and age.** You confirm that you have legal capacity to make a donation and that you are at least 18 years old.

## 3. What a Donation Is

**In short: a donation is a completed gift. The assets become the property of TheDAO when they arrive, and you keep no interest in them.**

**3.1 Nature of a donation.** When you donate to TheDAO, you agree that:

(a) a donation is a completed transfer of assets to TheDAO;

(b) donated assets become the property of TheDAO upon receipt;

(c) the donor retains no ownership or beneficial interest in the donated assets;

(d) donations are not deposits, investments, loans, escrowed funds, or funds held for the donor;

(e) TheDAO has discretion over how donated assets are deployed in furtherance of its nonprofit purposes;

(f) a donor has no contractual right to a refund; and

(g) TheDAO may reject, restrict, or return a donation when required or appropriate under its compliance policies or applicable law.

**3.2 Unconditional.** A donation is unconditional. It takes effect as a completed gift at the moment the transfer is confirmed on the Ethereum network, and no condition, reservation, or future event attaches to it.

**3.3 Selecting an Initiative.** Selecting an Initiative tells TheDAO where you want your donation applied. It does not make your donation conditional on that Initiative going ahead, reaching a funding goal, or finishing its work.

**3.4 No donor balances.** TheDAO does not maintain individual donor balances. TheDAO does not act as an escrow agent, a custodian, a trustee, or a financial services provider for any donor, and nothing in these Donation Terms creates such a relationship.

## 4. How Donations Work on the Site

**In short: Ethereum mainnet only, a fixed list of assets, small minimums, and one dedicated multisignature wallet per Initiative.**

**4.1 Network.** The Site accepts donations on Ethereum mainnet only (chain ID 1). TheDAO does not undertake to recover assets sent on any other network, including any Layer 2 network, sidechain, or test network.

**4.2 Supported assets.** The Site accepts native ETH and the following ERC-20 tokens: USDC, USDT, DAI, USDS, crvUSD, BOLD, fxUSD, EURC, and ZCHF. Each token contract is verified onchain before it is offered, and only verified tokens appear in the donate widget. Assets outside this list are not supported. TheDAO may add or remove supported assets at any time.

**4.3 Minimums.** The minimum donation is 0.0005 ETH for native ETH and one (1) whole token unit for each supported ERC-20 token.

**4.4 Destination.** Each Initiative has its own dedicated Gnosis Safe multisignature wallet requiring three (3) of five (5) signers. Donations go to the multisignature wallet of the Initiative you select, not to a central TheDAO wallet. The wallet configuration is verified onchain before an Initiative can receive donations.

**4.5 Attribution is best-efforts.** When you donate through the widget, the Site records the transaction hash and verifies the transfer onchain. TheDAO makes reasonable efforts to attribute other transfers, but attribution is not guaranteed. In particular, native ETH sent manually to an Initiative's multisignature wallet address without submitting the transaction hash through the Site may not be counted toward that Initiative's displayed progress and may not appear in the public donation list. **A transfer that is not attributed is still a donation, is still received by TheDAO, and is still governed by these Donation Terms.** Failure to display or credit a donation gives the donor no right of repayment and no other claim against TheDAO.

**4.6 USD figures are informational.** USD amounts shown on the Site are calculated for display only. ETH is valued using the Chainlink ETH/USD price feed, USD-denominated stablecoins are treated as 1:1, and EURC and ZCHF are valued using their own Chainlink price feeds. Prices move and feeds can be delayed or unavailable. The USD figure shown when you donate is not a representation of value or of what TheDAO ultimately applies to an Initiative.

**4.7 No accounts.** The Site does not offer donor accounts, logins, balances, or stored payment methods. You interact with it through your own wallet.

**4.8 Funding progress includes pledges.** Funding progress shown for an Initiative includes announced backer pledges that have not yet been paid. A pledge is shown as "pledged" until payment is received and as "received" after that. Progress figures are informational and are not a statement that the amounts shown have been received.

## 5. Use of Donations

**In short: TheDAO decides how donated assets are used. There is no right to repayment or refund.**

**5.1 Application of donated assets.** TheDAO applies donated assets toward the designated Initiative in its sole discretion, in furtherance of its nonprofit purposes. This includes the timing, sequencing, and amount of any payment to a vendor or grantee, and whether to make any payment at all.

**5.2 Initiatives that do not proceed.** If an Initiative does not proceed, does not reach its funding goal, is cancelled, or is not completed, TheDAO Security Fund reclaims the unspent funds and puts them toward other Ethereum security initiatives.

**5.3 No right to repayment; discretionary return.** **No donor has a right to repayment or refund and TheDAO may, in its sole discretion and pursuant to its applicable policies, return some or all of a donation in appropriate circumstances. Any amounts not returned remain the property of TheDAO and may be used for other purposes consistent with its nonprofit mission.**

**5.4 The discretion is genuine.** The discretion described in section 5.3 is genuine and is exercised case by case. It is not a policy, a program, or an expected outcome, and TheDAO does not operate any procedure through which a donor may request or obtain the return of a donation. Nothing TheDAO says or does in one case creates an entitlement, a precedent, or an expectation in any other case.

**5.5 Sanctions carve-out.** Section 5.3 does not apply where an asset is subject to a freezing obligation or where TheDAO otherwise determines that applicable law requires it. In that case the asset is frozen in place, is not used or moved, is documented, and is reported to the competent Marshall Islands authority. No return is made in those circumstances, and TheDAO may be unable to discuss the matter with you.

**5.6 No fee is deducted.** No part of a donation is deducted as a fee. Fees payable to TheDAO's round operator are paid by TheDAO from its own treasury.

**6.6 Screening creates no financial relationship.** Screening does not make TheDAO a bank, a virtual asset service provider, a money transmitter, or a financial institution, and TheDAO does not provide any account, custody, or money transmission service to donors.

**8.3 Pseudonymous, not anonymous.** Donations are pseudonymous rather than anonymous.
