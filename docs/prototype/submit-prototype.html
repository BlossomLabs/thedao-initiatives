The page fields These render in the header of your initiative page, next to the type badge. They are never part of a body section.
Backers already committed Optional, and open to every type. Anyone who has already committed money to this work, with their logo. The header of your page and your board card show the total and the logos, the way backer logos show on every initiative today.
The sections The site renders the heading, you answer the question. Fixed order, no renaming, no dropping. Bold, links and lists are welcome inside a field.
Milestones Each row renders as a heading with a checklist under it. The site letters them A, B, C in the order they sit here. The amounts add up against your funding goal.
Checks Warnings are yours to judge, you can submit past them. Errors do not block the button: press Submit and every problem is marked on the question it belongs to.
