Structure the legacy bodies

Initiatives submitted before the one-question-per-section form keep their text in one details body. This splits a body into the section columns the new pages render, but only where every required section is present, every milestone has an amount and criteria, the milestone amounts add up to the goal, and nothing is left over. Anything else stays as it is and says why: add the missing heading to the body on its manage page (the current approved initiatives mostly lack an "Out of scope" section), then come back. The body is kept in the row after conversion, so a wrong split is undone by clearing the structured flag. Apply takes an SQLite backup first (backups/rfps-<time>-pre-strip.db).
