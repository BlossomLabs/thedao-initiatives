How it works

1
The ecosystem writes RFPs

Specific security work with a budget: an idea, not a team. Anyone can submit one from a forum post.

2
Sponsors pledge, anyone donates

Companies pledge and pay only when the work ships. Donations land on-chain instantly and count as a public signal.

3
TheDAO completes the gap

The 200 ETHSecurity badge holders rank the RFPs that come up short, and TheDAO Security Fund finishes funding the top ones.
